Hello there !!!

I’m Arthur Lin, a UI/UX designer passionate about enhancing user interaction of website and app, creating high usable interface designs that deliver intuitive human–computer interaction experiences.

During the pandemic lockdown, I was introduced to Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, which opened up a whole new creative world for me to explore visual design. That short period of time sparked my journey into the design field.

I grew up in Taipei, Taiwan and I am currently studying at The University of Melbourne. The chance of living in Melbourne in Australia has opened up many opportunities and broadened my global perspective, motivating me to become the best designer I can be.

I’m excited to continue growing in the design field, and I’d love to share a collection of my work and experiences with you here in my space.
