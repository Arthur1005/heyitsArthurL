Book Jacket & NGV Exhibition Project

///

Designer

Sep 2025

Project Overview

///

Translating the atmosphere of an exhibition into a publication, this project explores the design of an exhibition catalogue and promotional system for an Impressionism exhibition at the National Gallery of Victoria. Working with existing artworks, the challenge was to create a visual direction that complemented the original pieces while establishing a cohesive identity through typography, layout and composition. Alongside the book jacket design, an official promotional poster was developed to extend the exhibition’s visual language across both print and public-facing materials.
