Brief / Introduction

///

Profile edit covers the redesign of Circula's Profile Editing screen — the page students use to update their name, institution, degree, pronouns, and contact details.

The existing screen had fallen out of step with the rest of the app: inconsistent margin and padding, several elements that no longer matched the design system, and privacy settings that had drifted onto the general settings page instead of living here. I led the UX work to rebuild the screen — clarifying how users discover editable fields, tightening it to the design system, and making sure changes are saved (or clearly not) before users leave the page.

Learning & Reflect

///

Design system discipline

Auditing every spacing and component choice against the design system took longer than expected, but it made the case for treating consistency as a first-class requirement, not a final polish step.

Small removals matter

Cutting the date of birth field and rewriting the cancel flow felt like minor edits, but user feedback showed they removed real points of hesitation — sometimes the highest-impact change is what you take out, not what you add.

Where settings belong

Moving privacy controls back onto the edit profile page meant re-examining how the rest of the settings area was structured, which turned a one-screen fix into a broader information-architecture conversation.

Feedback changes scope

Early testing on the profile picture and dropdown interactions surfaced confusion I hadn't anticipated, which pushed the timeline but ultimately shaped decisions I'm more confident standing behind.
